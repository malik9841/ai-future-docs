# Responsible AI



Responsible AI focuses on designing, deploying, and using artificial intelligence in ways that are reliable, transparent, secure, and aligned with human needs.



## Key principles



### Accuracy



AI-generated information should be evaluated before it is used in decisions or published as authoritative content.



### Transparency



Users should understand when AI is being used and, where appropriate, how its outputs are generated or evaluated.



### Privacy



AI systems should handle personal and sensitive information according to applicable privacy requirements and organizational policies.



### Human oversight



AI should support human decision-making rather than completely replacing appropriate human judgment.



## Why Responsible AI matters



Responsible AI helps organizations build trust, reduce risk, and use AI in ways that provide value while considering potential impacts on users and society.



## Conclusion



As AI becomes more integrated into products and workflows, responsible design and human oversight will remain essential.
